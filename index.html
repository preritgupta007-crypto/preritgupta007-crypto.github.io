FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Getting started

What is Athlomate?

Athlomate helps you find people to play your sport with: Athlomates near you, at a similar level. Join a game or create your own, then meet up and play.

How do skill levels work?

For each sport you pick Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced. Athlomate uses it to suggest Athlomates at a similar level. After an event, other Athlomates can confirm your level. You can change it any time from your profile.

Who can use Athlomate?

You must be 18 or older. Athlomate works everywhere in Greece: you choose your town or area, and you see players and games within the distance you pick.

Events

How do I join an event?

Open an event and tap "Request to join". The host approves or declines your request. If an event is full you can't request a spot. You can withdraw a request or leave an event any time from its page.

How do I create an event?

On the Events tab tap "Create event". Choose the sport, an open game or a challenge, a title, when and where, how many Athlomates you need (not counting you) and the level. Afterwards you can invite Athlomates, and edit or cancel the event from its page.

What is "Play now"?

If you host an event that starts within 24 hours and still has open spots, you can say you need Athlomates now. It then shows a "Play now" badge to Athlomates nearby who play that sport at a suitable level. It ends when the event starts, fills up, or you turn it off.

How do I invite friends?

Tap the share icon on your Profile to send your invite link. You can also share an event from its page, or send one of your events as a card in a chat.

Messages and chat

Who can message me?

Only your connections. To message someone, send them a short message request; once they accept, you can chat. People in the same game can always talk in the game chat. Nobody can message you after a block, in either direction.

Can I send photos?

Yes, in direct messages and in game chats. In a direct message you can't start a conversation with a photo: the other Athlomate has to write to you first. In a game chat, the host and the players can send photos any time the chat is open. Photos are resized and their location data is removed before they are sent.

Privacy and location

Does Athlomate track my location?

Only approximately, and only while the app is open, never in the background. When you choose your town, your phone can suggest the nearest one; only the town you pick is saved, never your position. If you choose to appear on the Discover map, your phone rounds your position to an area of about 2 km and only that area is stored. Turn it off in Settings and the stored area is deleted.

Who can see my profile and age?

Other Athlomates see your name, photo, town, sports, levels and number of connections. In Settings → Privacy you can hide your age or hide yourself from Discover. Read receipts ("Seen") work both ways: if either of you turns them off, neither of you sees "Seen".

Safety and reporting

How do I report or block someone?

Open their profile and tap the "…" menu to report or block. You can also report a single chat message, a game or a message request. If you block someone, you no longer see each other in Discover or Messages, any request or connection between you is removed, and neither of you can message the other. Manage this in Settings → Blocked users.

How can I stay safe when meeting Athlomates?

Meet in a public place, tell a friend where you are going, and trust your instincts. Athlomate doesn't handle payments or book courts. If someone makes you uncomfortable, report or block them.

Why do I need to connect before messaging?

To keep unwanted messages away, you send a short request first. Once the other person accepts, you're connected and can chat. A request that isn't answered expires after 30 days. Game chats work as before, and you can always report or block someone.

Account

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete account and confirm twice. Your profile, sports, connections, events you host, joined events and messages are permanently deleted. This can't be undone.
