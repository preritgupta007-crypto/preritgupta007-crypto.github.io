3. Legal bases

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): account, profile, games, chats and notifications, which are needed to provide the app.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): the approximate area on the map. It is off by default and you can switch it off at any time.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): safety, reports, blocking, preventing abuse, keeping the service secure, and finding and fixing bugs (diagnostic error reports). [REVIEW: confirm this is the right legal basis for diagnostic error reports]
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): when the law requires us to keep or hand over data.

4. Location

  • The app asks for location only while it is in use, with low accuracy, and never in the background.
  • When you pick your city, your device position is used once to suggest the nearest city. Only the city name is saved, not the position.
  • The approximate area is opt-in (off by default). Your device rounds the position to a grid of about 2 km and only the centre of that cell is sent and stored; the database rounds it again. We never store or send a precise position. Other users may see you on the map in that area and a rounded distance.
  • "My location" on a map only moves the map on your screen and stores nothing.

5. Photos

Profile photos are uploaded to our storage. Photos you send in chats are re-encoded on your device before upload (largest side 1600 px, JPEG) which removes hidden data such as EXIF and GPS location. Chat photos are kept in a private storage area and shown only to the people in that chat through short-lived links; the app does not keep them on your device's disk.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, have it deleted, restrict or object to its use, receive it in a portable form, and withdraw consent at any time (for example by switching off the approximate area). Most of this you can do directly in the app (Edit profile, Settings). To delete your account and all your data, use Settings, Delete account. For anything else write to SUPPORT_EMAIL; we answer within one month.

You may also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr) or to the authority in your own country.

10. Age

Athlomate is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think someone under 18 uses the app, please tell us and we will remove the account.

11. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, access is restricted with database rules so that people only see what the app lets them see, and chat photos are private. No system is perfectly secure; if there is a breach that affects you, we will inform you and the authority as the law requires.
