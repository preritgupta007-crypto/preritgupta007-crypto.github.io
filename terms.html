Terms of Service

These terms apply when you use the Athlomate app. By creating an account you agree to them. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. What Athlomate is

Athlomate helps people find others to play sport with in the same city and at a similar level. You can create and join games, chat with other players and give feedback after a game. Athlomate does not organise, supervise or insure any game, and it does not book courts or fields. Court information, including whether a court is free or paid, may be wrong. You are responsible for checking availability and for any booking or payment with the venue. It is not a dating app.

2. Who can use it (18+)

You must be 18 years or older. By signing up you confirm that you are. We may close accounts that we believe belong to someone under 18.

3. Your account

  • Give accurate information about yourself (name, age, city, sports and skill levels) and keep it up to date.
  • One person, one account. Keep your password private. You are responsible for what happens under your account.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings, Delete account). This is permanent.

4. How to behave: zero tolerance

Athlomate is for friendly, safe sport. We have zero tolerance for abusive content and abusive users. You must not:

  • harass, threaten, insult, discriminate against or pressure anyone, or send unwanted sexual content or advances;
  • post anything illegal, hateful, violent or sexually explicit, or share someone else's private information or photos without their consent;
  • impersonate another person, lie about your age or identity, or create fake or duplicate accounts;
  • use the app for spam, advertising, selling, or to recruit for anything other than playing sport;
  • try to break, overload or misuse the app or its data, or collect other users' data.

5. Report, block and how we moderate

You can report a user, a message or a game and block a user from the app at any time. A blocked user cannot see you or contact you. Reports are stored together with the information needed to review them.
